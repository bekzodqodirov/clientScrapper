## 1. Clearly allowed

1. **Company-tier data from state sources**: legal name, STIR/INN, OKPO, legal address, registration date, status, activity codes, participants — from ЕГРПО / my.gov.uz / orginfo.uz / MyOrg.uz. Legal-entity attributes are outside the PD definition ([nrm.uz](https://nrm.uz/contentf?doc=593113_zakon_respubliki_uzbekistan_ot_02_07_2019_g_n_zru-547_o_personalnyh_dannyh_%28prinyat_zakonodatelnoy_palatoy_16_04_2019_g_odobren_senatom_21_06_2019_g_%29&products=8_kadrovyy_konsultant)). The director's name taken from the registry is PD but has a public-source basis and the no-notification exemption ([lex.uz](https://www.lex.uz/acts/4396428)); store it with `source=registry`, purpose "B2B offer of freight services".
2. **Aggregated official trade statistics** (stat.uz by HS code/country/month; data.gov.uz GTK datasets) for targeting sectors/regions ([stat.uz](https://stat.uz/ru/ofitsialnaya-statistika/merchandise-trade), [olddata.gov.uz](https://olddata.gov.uz/ru/sphere/authority/143)).
3. **Contacts a company itself publishes as business contacts** on its own website, Telegram business channel bio, Instagram business profile (category + "contact" button), marketplace seller page: company phone, info@ e-mail, @company handle. Read individually, store only the company contact, and keep the source URL.
4. **Inbound leads** (website form, Telegram bot start, DM to GSR account): consent and purpose are captured at the door; existing LogisticsERP flow with "📞 Bog'landim" is fine; add an explicit consent flag.
5. **Replying to a public intent post** ("Xitoydan yuk kerak", "ищу карго из Китая", "контейнер Гуанчжоу–Ташкент") in a group where a GSR manager is a legitimate member, as a human-approved, in-thread or one-to-one reply to that post. This is a response to the subject's own request (closest to the "pre-contract steps at subject's request" ground), not advertising distribution to a subscriber. Keep it one touch, personalised, from the manager's own account, no template blast.
6. **Processing existing clients' data** (400+ client book) for contract performance; marketing to them still needs recorded consent under ЗРУ-776 Art. 31 unless they opted in — capture it once in the CRM.
7. **Hosting the lead database in Uzbekistan** (any provider) or in an adequacy-list country (Russia, EU, USA, UK, etc.) after 03.08.2026 ([yuz.uz](https://yuz.uz/ru/news/uzbekistan-utverdil-perechen-iz-49-stran-s-ravnotsennoy-zaitoy-personalnx-dannx)); biometric/genetic/telecom-subscriber data are not collected, so the localisation duty does not bite. Sending PD to Claude (Anthropic, USA) is a transfer to a listed country; still minimise (see §4).
8. **Lawful use of the agent's own LLM extraction** when the underlying data processing is lawful; the AI aggravation in КоАО 46-2 part 2 applies to *unlawful* processing only ([kun.uz](https://kun.uz/ru/29340887)).

| Grey zone | Why grey | Mitigation to build into the agent |

## 3. Avoid

- Mass unsolicited SMS/calls/Telegram DMs from templates; any outbound 18:00–09:00; auto-sending from managers' accounts without the owner's approval gate.
- Buying phone/contact databases of individuals; exporting Telegram group member lists; harvesting chat histories into datasets; fake/"warm-up" accounts; scraping 1688/Alibaba/LinkedIn.
- Storing passport, PINFL, date of birth, photos, geolocation, private-chat content, any data on minors or B2C consumers.
- Sending raw names/phones/handles to any LLM or SaaS outside the adequacy list; hosting PD in a non-listed country (mainland China status unknown).
- Quoting prospects their own customs declaration data; presenting purchased declaration-level data as GSR's "statistics".
- Marketing claims banned by the website content rules ("duty-free", "fastest", "100% guarantee") in any outreach text — ЗРУ-776 also sanctions improper advertising via КоАО 178-1.
- Reusing collected data for a new purpose (e.g., selling the lead list, car/EV import B2C campaigns) without new consent.

## 4. Compliance checklist for the agent (build-time requirements)

**Legal basis & documents**
- Operator = Imex services LLC. Issue an order appointing a PD-responsible person; adopt a "Порядок обработки персональных данных" covering clientScrapper, MIYA, LogisticsERP, TelegramCustoms; file one registration at pd.gov.uz for the "B2B lead database" (purpose, categories, sources, storage location, security measures) and amend it when the sending system goes live — before 01.11.2026 ([pd.gov.uz](https://pd.gov.uz/), [Reg. 71](https://lex.uz/docs/-4729730?ONDATE=05.04.2022)).
- Record per lead: `legal_basis ∈ {public_source, pre_contract_request, consent, contract}`, `source_url`, `collected_at`, `collector` (agent version/account), `consent_status ∈ {none, asked, given, refused}`, `consent_evidence` (message id/screenshot hash), `do_not_contact` flag. Push these into LogisticsERP `note` (the lead API keeps extra fields in `note`) and use `source=other` or request a new `agent` source key.

**Data fields**

| Store | Do not store |
|---|---|
| Company: name, STIR, OKPO, legal/actual address, activity, website, company phone/e-mail, @company handle, Instagram business handle, goods categories, HS groups, estimated volume band, shipment type, score, signals (type + URL + date) | Passport/PINFL, birth date, home address, personal e-mail unrelated to the business, photos, group member lists, full chat logs, declaration-level customs rows, financial data of the person, family/relations, any data on individuals who are not decision makers |
| Person (max one per company at first): full name, role, business phone, Telegram handle, source URL, consent fields | Personal mobile obtained from a non-public source; second/third personal numbers; social graph |

- Pseudonymise before LLM extraction: replace phone numbers and handles with tokens before the Claude call; send only post text + company-level context; reverse the mapping locally. Keep Haiku/batch usage caps as in MIYA.

**Retention**
- Intent posts not converted to a qualified lead: delete in 30 days. Qualified lead with no reply: 90 days then anonymise to company-level only. Refusal/opt-out: keep only a hashed phone/handle in a suppression list indefinitely. Client: contract period + statutory accounting retention (lawyer to confirm). Purchased datasets: delete raw files after scoring run; keep only derived band + provenance record.

**Opt-out / rights handling**
- Global suppression list shared by clientScrapper, MIYA outbox gate and LogisticsERP (`lost_reason=do_not_contact` → write to suppression). Honour "STOP/Bekor/Отписаться/to'xtat" and any manager-marked refusal within 24 h; stop all channels for that person and that company's channel if asked. Implement subject requests: access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing (Art. on subject rights; ryskiyeva) with a 10-working-day SLA (lawyer to set the statutory term).

**Sending rules (owner-approved sender)**
